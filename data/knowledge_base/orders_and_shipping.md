# Orders and shipping

## Tracking an order

Customers can find the latest available shipment status from the tracking link in their order confirmation or shipping email. Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to appear after a shipping notification is sent. If there is no tracking link or the tracking page has not updated after 24 hours, support should ask for the order number and investigate.

## Delivery estimates

The delivery estimate shown at checkout is an estimate, not a guaranteed arrival date. Carrier delays, weather, and local delivery conditions can change the actual delivery time. Support should check the order's current carrier status before giving an order-specific update.

## Updating a delivery address

An address change may be possible before an order is prepared for shipment. Once the carrier has the parcel, the customer should use the carrier's available delivery options. Support should not claim that an address was changed unless the order system confirms the update.
