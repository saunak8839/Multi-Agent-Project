# Billing and refund information

## Duplicate charges

If a customer sees two successful charges for one order, support should compare the payment records with the order total. A pending authorization is not the same as a completed charge. Customers should provide their order number and the dates and amounts shown by their payment provider so support can investigate.

## Refund timing

After a refund is approved and issued, the time it takes to appear depends on the customer's bank or card provider. The support desk should not promise a specific arrival date unless a verified payment record or provider policy supplies one. A customer can contact their payment provider if the refund has been issued but is not visible after the provider's stated processing window.

## Refund eligibility

This demo knowledge base does not define product-specific refund eligibility or authorize a refund. Support must check the applicable order and current company policy before making an eligibility decision. A support agent may collect details and explain the investigation process, but a refund must not be promised based only on this article.
